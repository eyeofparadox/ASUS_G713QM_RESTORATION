## **acronis windows 10 disk-, partition- management and backup**

---

Acronis True Image for Windows 10 **allows you to back up entire disks, specific partitions, or individual files and folders, as well as manage partitions**. You can create full system image backups, restore from backups, and clone disks. \[[1](https://care.acronis.com/s/article/Acronis-True-Image-how-to-back-up-files-or-disks?language=en_US), [1](https://care.acronis.com/s/article/Acronis-True-Image-how-to-back-up-files-or-disks?language=en_US), [2](https://www.acronis.com/en-us/blog/posts/full-backup/), [2](https://www.acronis.com/en-us/blog/posts/full-backup/), [3](https://www.acronis.com/en-us/support/documentation/ATI2025/7937.html), [3](https://www.acronis.com/en-us/support/documentation/ATI2025/7937.html), [4](https://care.acronis.com/s/article/Acronis-True-Image-how-to-back-up-entire-computer?language=en_US), [5](https://www.kingston.com/en/support/technical/acronis-download#:~:text=Acronis%20%C2%AE%20True%20Image%20for%20Kingston%20is,media%20from%20USB%20drives%20or%20CD/DVD%20discs.)\]

**Disk and Partition Management:**

* **Backup Source Selection:** When creating a backup, you can choose to back up entire disks, specific partitions, or individual files and folders. \[[1](https://care.acronis.com/s/article/Acronis-True-Image-how-to-back-up-files-or-disks?language=en_US), [2](https://www.acronis.com/en-us/blog/posts/full-backup/), [3](https://www.acronis.com/en-us/support/documentation/ATI2025/7937.html)\]  
* **Partition Manager:** Acronis Disk Director 12 is a part of Acronis True Image that allows you to manage partitions, including cloning disks to replacement hard drives. \[[6](https://www.acronis.com/en-us/blog/posts/partition-manager/#:~:text=Once%20deleted%20or%20lost%20partitions%20are%20found%2C,recovering%20masses%20of%20data%20that%20you%20don't.), [7](https://www.acronis.com/en-gb/blog/posts/partition-manager/)\]  
* **Clone Disks:** Acronis Disk Director 12 can clone disks to replacement hard drives, but it only supports cloning basic MBR disks, not dynamic or GPT disks. \[[7](https://www.acronis.com/en-gb/blog/posts/partition-manager/)\]  
* **Restore Partitions:** You can restore individual partitions or disks from backups, including from Acronis Cloud or local storage. \[[8](https://www.acronis.com/en-eu/blog/posts/full-backup/)\]

**Backup Options:**

* **Backup Destination:** You can choose local folders, external hard drives, NAS devices, or the Acronis Cloud as backup destinations. \[[1](https://care.acronis.com/s/article/Acronis-True-Image-how-to-back-up-files-or-disks?language=en_US), [1](https://care.acronis.com/s/article/Acronis-True-Image-how-to-back-up-files-or-disks?language=en_US), [2](https://www.acronis.com/en-us/blog/posts/full-backup/), [2](https://www.acronis.com/en-us/blog/posts/full-backup/), [3](https://www.acronis.com/en-us/support/documentation/ATI2025/7937.html), [3](https://www.acronis.com/en-us/support/documentation/ATI2025/7937.html)\]  
* **Backup Types:** Acronis True Image offers full system image backups, where you can back up your entire computer. \[[4](https://care.acronis.com/s/article/Acronis-True-Image-how-to-back-up-entire-computer?language=en_US), [4](https://care.acronis.com/s/article/Acronis-True-Image-how-to-back-up-entire-computer?language=en_US)\]  
* **Backup Scheduling:** You can set up scheduled backups, including options for daily, weekly, or monthly backups. \[[1](https://care.acronis.com/s/article/Acronis-True-Image-how-to-back-up-files-or-disks?language=en_US), [1](https://care.acronis.com/s/article/Acronis-True-Image-how-to-back-up-files-or-disks?language=en_US), [2](https://www.acronis.com/en-us/blog/posts/full-backup/), [2](https://www.acronis.com/en-us/blog/posts/full-backup/), [3](https://www.acronis.com/en-us/support/documentation/ATI2025/7937.html), [3](https://www.acronis.com/en-us/support/documentation/ATI2025/7937.html), [9](https://www.acronis.com/en-us/products/true-image/backup/#:~:text=Scheduled%20backups:%20You%20can%20set%20up%20automatic,data%2C%20Acronis%20True%20Image%20makes%20it%20easy.), [10](https://www.acronis.com/en-gb/products/true-image/features/event-based-backup/), [11](https://www.acronis.com/en-us/products/true-image/features/reliability/#:~:text=Can%20I%20schedule%20automatic%20backups%20with%20Acronis,I%20back%20up%20using%20Acronis%20True%20Image?)\]  
* **Backup Options:** You can customize backup options like scheme settings, archive cleanup, and more. \[[1](https://care.acronis.com/s/article/Acronis-True-Image-how-to-back-up-files-or-disks?language=en_US), [1](https://care.acronis.com/s/article/Acronis-True-Image-how-to-back-up-files-or-disks?language=en_US)\]

**Restore Functionality:**

* **Restoring from Backup:** You can restore from backups created by Acronis True Image, including system images, individual disks, and partitions.  
* **Restoring from Cloud:** If you've backed up to the Acronis Cloud, you can restore from there by signing into your account.  
* **Restoring from Local Storage:** When restoring from local storage, ensure the backup drive is connected to your PC. \[[8](https://www.acronis.com/en-eu/blog/posts/full-backup/)\]
