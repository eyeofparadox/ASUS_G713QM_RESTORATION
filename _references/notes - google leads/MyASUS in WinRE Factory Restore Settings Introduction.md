## **MyASUS in WinRE Factory Restore Settings Introduction**

---

MyASUS in WinRE (Windows Recovery Environment) **provides a way to restore an ASUS computer to its factory settings**, offering a quick and accessible solution for users. It allows users to troubleshoot hardware problems and, if needed, restore the computer to its original state. This process can be initiated through the MyASUS application within WinRE. \[[1](https://www.asurion.com/connect/tech-tips/reset-asus-laptop/), [1](https://www.asurion.com/connect/tech-tips/reset-asus-laptop/), [2](https://www.asus.com/content/myasus-in-winre/#:~:text=MyASUS%20in%20WinRE%20is%20an%20easy%2Dto%2Daccess%20diagnostic,help%20you%20identify%20and%20troubleshoot%20hardware%20problems.), [2](https://www.asus.com/content/myasus-in-winre/#:~:text=MyASUS%20in%20WinRE%20is%20an%20easy%2Dto%2Daccess%20diagnostic,help%20you%20identify%20and%20troubleshoot%20hardware%20problems.), [3](https://www.asus.com/support/faq/1047855/), [3](https://www.asus.com/support/faq/1047855/), [4](https://www.asus.com/se/support/faq/1041621/#:~:text=Liknande%20%C3%A4mnen%20Applicable%20Products:%20Notebook%2C%20Desktop.%20ASUS,are%20to%20be%20removed%20through%20the%20process.)\]

Accessing MyASUS in WinRE: \[[2](https://www.asus.com/content/myasus-in-winre/#:~:text=MyASUS%20in%20WinRE%20is%20an%20easy%2Dto%2Daccess%20diagnostic,help%20you%20identify%20and%20troubleshoot%20hardware%20problems.), [3](https://www.asus.com/support/faq/1047855/), [5](https://www.asus.com/support/faq/1041621/#:~:text=Repeatedly%20clicking%20F12%20key%20on%20the%20keyboard,please%20try%20again%20to%20boot%20up%20notebook.), [6](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ONqLpoA-M3A)\]**

* To enter WinRE, you'll need to boot your computer and repeatedly press the F12 key, or the appropriate function key for your specific ASUS model (e.g., F9). This will lead you to the "Choose an option" interface.  
* From there, select "Troubleshoot" and then "MyASUS in WinRE". \[[3](https://www.asus.com/support/faq/1047855/), [3](https://www.asus.com/support/faq/1047855/), [5](https://www.asus.com/support/faq/1041621/#:~:text=Repeatedly%20clicking%20F12%20key%20on%20the%20keyboard,please%20try%20again%20to%20boot%20up%20notebook.), [5](https://www.asus.com/support/faq/1041621/#:~:text=Repeatedly%20clicking%20F12%20key%20on%20the%20keyboard,please%20try%20again%20to%20boot%20up%20notebook.), [6](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ONqLpoA-M3A), [6](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ONqLpoA-M3A)\]

**2\. Factory Restore Options within MyASUS:**

* Once in MyASUS, navigate to the "System Diagnosis" section and explore the available options. \[[7](https://www.asus.com/us/support/faq/1041680/), [7](https://www.asus.com/us/support/faq/1041680/)\]  
* You can perform diagnostic tests to check the health of various components, including the operating system. \[[7](https://www.asus.com/us/support/faq/1041680/), [7](https://www.asus.com/us/support/faq/1041680/)\]  
* If a factory reset is needed, look for the "Reset my PC" option within the "Diagnostic History". \[[7](https://www.asus.com/us/support/faq/1041680/), [7](https://www.asus.com/us/support/faq/1041680/)\]  
* Follow the on-screen instructions to either remove everything (deleting all files, apps, and settings) or keep your files (resets apps and settings only). \[[1](https://www.asurion.com/connect/tech-tips/reset-asus-laptop/), [1](https://www.asurion.com/connect/tech-tips/reset-asus-laptop/)\]

**3\. Troubleshooting and Support:**

* MyASUS in WinRE provides a suite of diagnostic tools to help identify and resolve potential hardware issues. \[[2](https://www.asus.com/content/myasus-in-winre/#:~:text=MyASUS%20in%20WinRE%20is%20an%20easy%2Dto%2Daccess%20diagnostic,help%20you%20identify%20and%20troubleshoot%20hardware%20problems.), [2](https://www.asus.com/content/myasus-in-winre/#:~:text=MyASUS%20in%20WinRE%20is%20an%20easy%2Dto%2Daccess%20diagnostic,help%20you%20identify%20and%20troubleshoot%20hardware%20problems.)\]
